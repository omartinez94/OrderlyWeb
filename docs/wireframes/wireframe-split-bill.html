Annotation pins — what to look at, and why

1 Grand-totals always visible · tax breakdown

A black "command-bar" sticky at the top of the items column shows the original $42.90 subtotal, the VIP discount, and the running reconciliation against what each seat is now liable for. Goal: cashier never loses sight of the original total. Tax per-seat computed at 10% on assigned items only.

2 Seat card anatomy · drop target with live recalc

Each seat is a card with: avatar+name, optional discount badge, scrollable drop-zone, per-seat totals, payment actions. Drop target highlights on drag-over (cyan border, dashed, scale 1.01). Touches receive safe drop behavior — no item "loss" even if dropped outside.

3 Per-seat modifier visibility

Modifiers carry with the item when dragged (the "+ tomato" badge follows the Bruschetta). Critical for allergy tracking — if Hernández is allergic and ordered the Bruschetta with no pesto, dragging it to another guest without warning would be a real safety issue. v2: drag-with-allergy-check modal.

4 Unassigned zone · the "honesty" area

Items that the table hasn't decided yet (e.g. "who had the espresso?") live in this dashed-border zone at the top. The cashier or waiter can see "1 item unassigned · drag to a seat" as a status indicator. Empty = "all bills assigned and ready".

5 Quick actions · Split evenly · Clear · Add seat

Three contextual actions: Split evenly distributes unassigned items equally across all seats (the "round-number way out"); Clear undoes all assignments back to unassigned; Add seat expands to 5, 6, … seats when there are more diners than the manifest. VIP discount on Seat 1 is sticky — applies to Seat 1's items even after re-distribution.

Interactivity — what's working & what to test

✓ Working in this wireframe
  • HTML5 drag-drop: items ↔ seats ↔ unassigned zone
  • Live per-seat subtotal + tax + total recalculation
  • Grand-total reconciliation at top
  • Split evenly — distributes unassigned items round-robin
  • Clear all, Add seat, Mark paid, Print (visual only)
⚠ To test in dev sprint
  • Touch — native long-press on mobile (no HTML5 drag)
  • Keyboard a11y — tab + space to pick up, arrows to move, space to drop
  • Modifier carry — when an item with allergy mods is dragged, show a confirm modal
  • Network — debounce PATCH on each drop (avoid one network call per drag)
  • Realtime — what if another waiter edits the same order? Show "modified by …" toast + relink
